Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025  ·  Version 1.0

📦 What we collect

SereniTrack is designed to collect as little as possible. We store only the following two pieces of data, entirely inside your own browser — nothing is sent to any server.

  • Anonymous usage count — a single number that tracks how many suggestions you have used today. This resets automatically at midnight and is never linked to any personal identifier.
  • Consent record — a simple flag (true/false) confirming that you have read and agreed to these terms. This is stored so the consent modal does not appear on every visit.

🚫 What we do NOT collect

We want to be explicit. SereniTrack does not collect, store, transmit, or process any of the following:

Your name  ·  Email address  ·  Phone number  ·  IP address  ·  Location  ·  Mood text you type  ·  Health or medical data  ·  Biometric data  ·  Device fingerprint

The mood text you enter is sent directly to the AI model to generate your suggestion and is then discarded. It is never stored in our systems or your browser.

⏱️ How long we keep it

  • The daily usage counter resets automatically every midnight. After the reset, the previous count is gone permanently.
  • The consent flag has no expiry — it persists in your browser until you clear your browser storage or uninstall the app.
  • Nothing is stored on any external server. There is no database, no account, no cloud backup. If you clear your browser data, all SereniTrack data is gone.

⚖️ Your rights

Because all data lives exclusively in your browser, you are always in full control.

  • View your data — open your browser's DevTools (F12) → Application → Local Storage → find serenitrack_usage and serenitrack_consent.
  • Delete your data — clear the two keys above in DevTools, or go to your browser Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data → Cached data and cookies.
  • Withdraw consent — delete the serenitrack_consent key from Local Storage. The consent modal will reappear on your next visit.

✉️ Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or how your data is handled, you can reach the SereniTrack team at:
